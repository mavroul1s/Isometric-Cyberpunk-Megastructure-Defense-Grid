SECTOR 07 — GRID EXPANSION

ANANKE DEFENSE SYSTEMS // OPERATOR BRIEFING
YOUR PURPOSE. You are the operator of a cyberpunk megacity sector. You own a ring of land around a central spire. Every structure you build earns credits ⏣ — and hostile intrusions are constantly trying to drain those credits out of your account.

YOUR GOAL. Spend credits to expand the sector outward through 6 rings and fill every plot. Build fast enough to out-earn what the breaches steal from you.

THE LOOP

EARN
Nodes pay out per second. Click the core spire to harvest, and grab ◆ data caches that ride the transit tubes.
SPEND
Build new nodes, upgrade them to tier 5, add firewalls, buy drones.
EXPAND
Click the core spire → ⊕ EXPAND to claim the next ring. That is how you win.
DEFEND
Click a breached node repeatedly to purge it before its timer runs out.

NODE CLASSES

⚡ REACTOR
Makes power. Everything else consumes it — run out and all yield throttles.
⛨ BASTION
Gives +2 firewall to every node inside its green ring.
↑ SPIRE
+15% yield to each node it links to.
⬡ ARCOLOGY
Huge yield, huge power draw. ▮ MONOLITH earns more but attracts breaches.
LINKS
Nodes wired by tubes earn +8% each. An isolated node loses 25% — build close together.

THREATS

LEECH
Drains credits. Firewalls block most of it.
WORM
Spreads to a linked node if you let it time out.
BLACKOUT
Zeroes the node's income and ignores firewalls entirely — drones are the answer.
GHOST
Invisible in Tactical Cyan. If credits drain with nothing on screen, switch optics to find it.
